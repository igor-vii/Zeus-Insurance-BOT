## Architectural Decisions

### Immutable Contract vs Mutable Commercial Layer

> Pricing MUST NOT be embedded as an immutable smart-contract parameter unless
> absolutely required by settlement semantics.

- Smart contracts define: authorization, ownership, settlement, accounting invariants, fund movement, cryptographic verification
- Off-chain configuration defines: price per operation, packages, discounts, quotas, billing policies, service tiers
- Changing commercial pricing must NOT require smart-contract redeployment

### Evidence Sufficiency and Claimability (Future Principle)

> Uncertainty is an operational input, not missing data.
> When evidence is insufficient to support a consequential claim,
> Zeus must preserve UNKNOWN and block or defer the dependent action
> rather than manufacture certainty.

See `ROADMAP_2026-08-29.md` section #14 for full principle definition.

### Temporal Trust Boundary (Future Research)

See `ROADMAP_2026-08-29.md` section #13 for temporal trust boundary research scope.

## Acceptance Criterion for Secretariat Production Readiness

> Can an independent external agent use Secretariat as a non-custodial economic
> service and receive a durable, verifiable operational record of what happened?

This requires resolving:

- External payment authorization (non-custodial signer)
- Two-phase payment lifecycle (request -> sign -> submit)
- Production composition root (shared stores, engine, worker)
- HTTP API boundary
- Reconciliation worker production lifecycle
- Recovery (recoverAfterCrash at startup)
- Evidence completeness and machine readability
- Billing identity and usage metering

---

## Architectural Freeze for Higher Layers

Until Secretariat completes its controlled production pilot:

### DO NOT

- Merge Insurance into Secretariat's critical path
- Make Escrow a prerequisite for Secretariat
- Introduce insurance-specific settlement logic into Secretariat
- Embed commercial pricing into immutable contracts
- Design final insurance pricing from theoretical assumptions
- Build Reputation as an independent free-feedback system

### DO

- Strengthen Secretariat core
- Make evidence machine-readable
- Accumulate operational data
- Preserve failures and UNKNOWN states
- Measure real economics
- Establish non-custodial authorization
- Build billing boundary independently from settlement contracts

---

## Strategic Objective

> **Make Secretariat independently useful, independently trustworthy and independently profitable.**

Once achieved, Secretariat becomes the foundation from which remaining Zeus layers
can be built using empirical operational data rather than assumptions.
